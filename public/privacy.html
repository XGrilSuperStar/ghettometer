Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy explains what information Gettometer ("we", "us") handles when you use this website. We keep it simple: there are no accounts, and we do not sell personal information.

Information we do not ask for

Gettometer does not ask you to register, log in, or provide your name, email address or payment details to use the map.

Information handled when you use the map

  • Server logs. Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically record technical information such as IP address, browser type, pages requested and the time of the request. This is used for security, troubleshooting and basic traffic measurement.
  • Address searches. When you type an address or city into the search box, the text is sent to the OpenStreetMap Nominatim geocoding service to find its location. Their handling of that request is governed by the OpenStreetMap Foundation's privacy policy.
  • Map tiles. Your browser requests map images from the USGS National Map, and, if you use the 3D view, map data from OpenFreeMap. Those services receive your IP address as part of the request.
  • Pin location. The spot you click is used in your browser to calculate nearby counts. We do not store it on a server.
  • Contact messages. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and we use it only to reply.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners are third-party vendors that use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info. To learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Gettometer does not control cookies set by advertisers. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, although parts of the web may work differently if you do.

Residents of the EEA, UK and Switzerland

Where required by law, we request your consent for personalized advertising cookies through a consent message provided by Google. You can change your choice at any time through that message or your browser settings.

California privacy rights

California residents may have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To make a request, use the Contact page. Opting out of personalized ads is described above.

Data we show about places

The map displays public information about businesses, parks, transit stops and neighborhoods drawn from open and government sources. It does not display information about private individuals.

Children

Gettometer is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS where supported by the hosting platform. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Third-party links

This site links to outside websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Questions about this policy? Reach us through the Contact page.
